<H3>Introduction</H3>
<P>
For 10 days in July and August 2001 I was part of team three of
Earthwatch's Sea Otters of Alaska project. We spent most of the time
on the water in small skiffs, monitoring the otters and surveying the
study area as part of a long-term project.
<p>
The goals of the project are a long-term study into the movements and
habitat use of sea otters. This website relates my experience with the
team. I'm also going to edit the video I took into a coherent film of
the trip. The photographs on these pages are all stills from the video.
</P>
