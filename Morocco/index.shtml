Morocco 2000



Across Europe to Africa

In August 2000, Sam took his Land Rover, Elsa, to Morocco, with me and Abigail. Most of the content on this website is in my daily diary of the trip, but there is also plenty of information on the team.

It was an eye-opening and enjoyable trip for all of us. We stayed at campsites, service station car parks, one hotel, and two wild camps in the middle of nowhere. In total we covered over 9500 km in 29 days. Over the four weeks we had our good times and our bad times, but we all had an experience we'll never forget.
